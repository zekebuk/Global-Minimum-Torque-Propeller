This script implements a reduced-order inverse geometry solver for a GMT-inspired propeller. The solver minimizes a weighted objective consisting of thrust error, torque, spanload-shape error, smoothness, geometric bounds, and optional reference-geometry regularization. In the reference-regularized mode used for the manufactured propeller, chord distribution and airfoil schedule were constrained to the selected manufacturable reference geometry, while twist was adjusted to match the GMT-inspired inboard-loaded spanload and target thrust condition. When the reduced-order reference-aero calibration is active, the scalar section-force scale is reported in the solver summary rather than hidden in the geometry. Therefore, the resulting geometry should be interpreted as a calibrated reproduction of the published GMT propeller methodology rather than an independent high-fidelity aerodynamic optimum.
