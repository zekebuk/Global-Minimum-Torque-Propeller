## Assumptions and limits

- Polar model is a fit to XFOIL values recorded at the locked operating points (alpha 1.0-8.2 deg,
  Re 88220-237224). Outside that range the soft stall (Cl_max = 1.15) is an assumption, not XFOIL data.
- Hover only, no swirl, Prandtl tip loss with 0.05 floor, same as the locked solver.
- All results are model-to-model; none of this is experimental or CFD validation.
