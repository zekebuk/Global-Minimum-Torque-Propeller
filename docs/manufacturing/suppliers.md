3. Coating: epoxy coat 10-50 stators with masked bore (if not done by the lamination vendor).
4. CNC: bell (DWG-006) and base (DWG-007) at qty 50 and 500, 7075-T6, anodised, with the drawn tolerances.
5. Shaft (DWG-008): custom turned, hardened, ground Ø4 h5/Ø5 k6 with DIN 6799 groove, flat and M5 thread; 684ZZ (EZO/NMB) at 1,000-10,000 pcs from an authorised distributor.
